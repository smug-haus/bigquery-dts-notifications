```card

tags: [set, subset]
---
Q: Write the definition of `Subset s t` for `s t : Set α`.
A: `∀ a, a ∈ s → a ∈ t` — every element of `s` is also an element of `t`.
```

```card
id: srs-1.1-image-vs-preimage
module: "1.1"
tags: [function, image]
---
Q: Give the definitions of `image f s` and `preimage f t`.
A:
  - `image f s = {b | ∃ a, a ∈ s ∧ f a = b}`
  - `preimage f t = {a | f a ∈ t}`
Note the image quantifies existentially over the domain; the preimage
pulls back a codomain set into the domain.
```

```card
id: srs-1.1-inj-def
module: "1.1"
tags: [function, injective]
---
Q: Define `Injective f`.
A: `∀ {a₁ a₂}, f a₁ = f a₂ → a₁ = a₂`. Equal outputs force equal inputs.
```

```card
id: srs-1.1-surj-def
module: "1.1"
tags: [function, surjective]
---
Q: Define `Surjective f`.
A: `∀ b, ∃ a, f a = b`. Every codomain element has a preimage.
```

---
```card
Q: Name the constructors of `ReflTransClosure r`.
A: Two constructors:
  - `refl (a : α) : ReflTransClosure r a a`
  - `tail {a b c} : ReflTransClosure r a b → r b c → ReflTransClosure r a c`
```

```card
id: srs-1.1-rtc-as-lfp
module: "1.1"
tags: [relation, fixed-point]
---
Q: State the fixed-point characterization of the reflexive-transitive
   closure.
A: It is the least fixed point of `F(R) = Δ ∪ r ∪ R∘R` on the lattice
   of relations, where `Δ` is the diagonal. Monotonicity of `F` plus
   completeness of the lattice gives existence via Knaster–Tarski.
```

```card
id: srs-1.1-demorgan-constructive
module: "1.1"
tags: [logic, constructive]
---
Q: Why does the constructive proof of `¬(P ∧ Q) → ¬P ∨ ¬Q` fail?
A: From a non-pair we cannot constructively produce a witness for which
   specific conjunct fails. The classical `by_cases` on `P` (i.e.,
   `Classical.em P`) is required.
```
