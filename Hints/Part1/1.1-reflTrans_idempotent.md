## Level 1 — Nudge

Induct on the outer closure.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- The hypothesis is `ReflTransClosure (ReflTransClosure r) a b`.
- Induct on it. Two cases:
  - `refl a`: conclude `ReflTransClosure r a a` with `ReflTransClosure.refl`.
  - `tail _ hstep ih`: `hstep` is itself a `ReflTransClosure r`; the
    IH gives `ReflTransClosure r` up to the previous hop. Compose them
    with `reflTransClosure_trans` (a worked-example theorem from the
    module).

## Level 3 — Skeleton

```lean
intro a b h
induction h with
| refl a => sorry
| tail _ hstep ih => sorry   -- use reflTransClosure_trans
```
