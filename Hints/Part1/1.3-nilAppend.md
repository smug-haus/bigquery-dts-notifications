## Level 1 — Nudge

Definitional — `List.append`'s first case.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- `List.append` is defined by recursion on its *first* argument.
- The `[]` case returns the second argument unchanged.
- So `[] ++ xs = xs` is `rfl`.
