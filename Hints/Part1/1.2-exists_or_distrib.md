## Level 1 — Nudge

Pull the witness out, then split on the disjunction.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- The hypothesis is `∃ x, A x ∨ B x`. Destructure: `⟨x, h⟩`.
- `h : A x ∨ B x`. `cases` gives two branches.
- In each branch, commit to the matching outer disjunct and supply
  the witness `x`.

## Level 3 — Skeleton

```lean
intro ⟨x, h⟩
cases h with
| inl ha => exact Or.inl sorry
| inr hb => exact Or.inr sorry
```
