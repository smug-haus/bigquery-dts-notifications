## Level 1 — Nudge

Two negations, one hypothesis.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- Given `h : ¬(P ∨ Q)`, you owe `¬P ∧ ¬Q`.
- `¬P` is `P → False`; similarly for `¬Q`.
- Produce each by feeding the corresponding disjunct into `h`.
