## Level 1 — Nudge

`n * 0` reduces to `0` by definition.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- `Nat.mul` is defined by recursion on its second argument.
- The `0` case of the match returns `0`.
- Therefore `n * 0 = 0` is definitional — no induction.
