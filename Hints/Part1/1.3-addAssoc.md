## Level 1 — Nudge

Induct on the last variable — `k`. Addition reduces on its right.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- Base case `k = 0`: both sides reduce to `n + m` by `Nat.add`
  definitional equality.
- Inductive step `k = j.succ`: use `Nat.add_succ` to push `.succ`
  outside the parentheses on both sides, then apply the IH.

## Level 3 — Skeleton

```lean
induction k with
| zero => sorry
| succ j ih => sorry  -- use Nat.add_succ three times + ih
```
