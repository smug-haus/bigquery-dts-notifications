## Level 1 — Nudge

Iff is two implications.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- `constructor` splits the iff into the two directions.
- Forward: from `∀ x, A x ∧ B x`, produce two universals by
  projecting at each `x`.
- Backward: from `(∀ x, A x) ∧ (∀ x, B x)`, destructure and pair up
  at each `x`.
