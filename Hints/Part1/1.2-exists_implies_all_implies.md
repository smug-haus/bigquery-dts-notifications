## Level 1 — Nudge

You have a witness *and* a universal. Combine them.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- Destructure `∃ x, A x` into `⟨x, hx⟩`.
- Take the second hypothesis `f : ∀ x, A x → P`.
- `f x hx : P`.

## Level 3 — Skeleton
