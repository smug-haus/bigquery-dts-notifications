## Level 1 — Nudge

`∨` has two constructors; elimination is a case-split.

## Level 2 — Sketch

- Introduce the hypothesis `h : P ∨ Q`.
- Case-split with `cases h with | inl hp => _ | inr hq => _`.
- In each branch, commit to the other disjunct: `Or.inr hp` or
  `Or.inl hq`.

## Level 3 — Skeleton

```lean
intro h
cases h with
| inl hp => sorry
| inr hq => sorry
```
